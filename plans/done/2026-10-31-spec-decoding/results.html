qwen38-q6 output speed: mtp-ngram (5 drafts) vs baseline

Real lobo up path on RTX 5090 SECURE pods: image 0.2.4 (before) vs 0.2.5 (after). The same Q6_K weights, llama.cpp b11118 and ctx 65,536. The only change is speculative decoding: the model's built-in MTP head plus n-gram lookup in the context. Median of 2 reps.

TL;DR

  • Ships: LOBO_SPEC=mtp-ngram (default), MTP_DRAFT_MAX=5.
  • Speed: 1.8× on free prose, 2.7× on code, 4.2–4.8× on long-doc answers, 9.3× on code edits.
  • Quality: the 27B checks every draft token. Code, edit and long-doc outputs are byte-identical to the baseline.
  • Cost: +1.5 GB VRAM (24.9 of 32 GB). No new download. All measurements cost $1.60.

Quality

  • llama-server keeps a draft token only when the 27B samples the same token itself. Speculation changes speed, not the model.
  • ✓ W2–W5 outputs are byte-identical (SHA-256) to the baseline.
  • ⚠ W1 prose differs. The likely cause is float noise from verifying a batch of tokens: ngram W1 matched the baseline on one rep and not on the next, with the same prompt. ~80% sure; the texts were not diffed.
  • ✓ W6 mismatches came from random tool-call ids in the hash (Rival finding, fixed). Both after-run W6 reps are identical, and the tool call parses.

Cost of the change

  • VRAM: 23.4 GB → 24.9 GB (+1.5 GB) on the 32 GB card.
  • Time to first token: W4 4.76 s → 5.17 s (+9%) on the 25k-token prompt. Short prompts did not change.
  • No extra weights: the MTP head is already in the Q6_K GGUF.
  • Spend: $1.41 sweep pod + $0.12 before pod + $0.07 after pod = $1.60 of the $10 budget.
